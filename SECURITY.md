# Security Policy

Do More Soft takes the security of its software and infrastructure seriously. This document applies to all public and private repositories under the `Do-More-Soft` organization.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

**Do not** open a public issue for security vulnerabilities.

Please report security issues privately by email: **[contact@domoresoft.com](mailto:contact@domoresoft.com)**

Include, where possible:

- A description of the vulnerability and its potential impact.
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept.
- The affected repository, product, or version.
- Your contact details if you would like a response.

## What to Expect

- **Acknowledgement** within 3 business days.
- **Initial assessment** within 10 business days.
- **Fix timeline** depending on severity — critical issues are prioritized.
- **Coordinated disclosure** — we will agree on a public disclosure window together with the reporter.

## Scope

This policy covers:

- All code repositories under the `Do-More-Soft` organization.
- Do More Soft owned products and platforms (MoreSales, Kwadracik, CalMedic, Textio, MyCarApp, MoreNFC, MoreMobile, MoreCRM, MoreGenerator, GetMeCon, and others listed on [domoresoft.com](https://domoresoft.com/)).
- The `domoresoft.com` website and subdomains.

Issues in third-party dependencies should be reported to their respective maintainers, with a copy to us if they affect our products.

## Proprietary and Patent-Protected Software

Some repositories (for example [SmartVet](https://github.com/Do-More-Soft/SmartVet)) are marked as proprietary and patent-pending. Security reports are welcome under the same policy, but all other usage, redistribution, or derivative work is prohibited without a written agreement.

## Recognition

We credit researchers who report valid issues in coordinated disclosure. Monetary rewards are considered case by case for critical issues in production systems.
